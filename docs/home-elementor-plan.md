# RetreatVenues Home - Elementor Build Plan

## Direction

The home page should feel like a premium discovery/search experience, inspired by Bivomoto's rhythm without copying its brand: full image hero, strong search capsule, immediate venue proof, then destinations and conversion sections.

Keep WPRentals as the dynamic engine when possible. Elementor should handle the layout, spacing, copy, and visual hierarchy.

## Brand

- Main violet: `#705463`
- Hover violet: `#5C4351`
- Beige background: `#EDE6E2`
- Cream background: `#FBF7F4`
- Main text: `#2F292C`
- Secondary text: `#756B70`
- Green accent: `#7B8A73`
- Font: Inter
- Corners: 8px for cards, 999px for pill buttons/search

- Dark overlay.
- Content centered.
- Search capsule below the title.

- Eyebrow: `RETREAT VENUES WORLDWIDE`

- Search fields: `Destination`, `Retreat type`, `Guests`

- Search capsule becomes one column on mobile.

## Section 7 - How It Works

- H2: `Find, compare, contact and promote your retreat.`
- Text: `The process stays simple for organizers: choose the right space, confirm availability, message the owner, then prepare the retreat.`

Steps:
1. `Find a venue`
   `Browse the directory or use filters for country, venue type, capacity, amenities and retreat style.`
2. `Check availability`
   `Add your dates and guest number to understand availability, pricing and practical fit.`
3. `Send a message`
   `Describe your retreat plans and needs so the venue owner can respond with relevant details.`
4. `Confirm with the venue`
   `Continue directly with the venue team to agree dates, deposit, meals, rooms and logistics.`
5. `Promote the retreat`
   `After booking, send RetreatVenues your retreat details so the platform can help promote it.`
