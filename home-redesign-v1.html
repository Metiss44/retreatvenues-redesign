Planning guide

Choose the venue that fits the retreat, not just the photo.

Use these practical checks to avoid expensive mistakes and focus on venues that fit the real needs of the retreat.

Before you search, define the non-negotiables.

Most venue searches go wrong because organizers start with beautiful images instead of the real constraints of the retreat.

  • How many people are actually coming?
  • What is the real budget per person?
  • What does the group absolutely need on site?
  • How far can the venue be from a major airport?

What to compare before sending an inquiry.

A retreat venue needs to work operationally: rooms, food, staff, transport, quiet spaces, backup plans and the right atmosphere.

  • Capacity, bedrooms, bathrooms and retreat room setup.
  • Food quality, meal flexibility and dietary needs.
  • Reliable WiFi, basic AV, outlets and phone reception.
  • Cancellation policy, deposit terms and hidden fees.
  • Responsiveness from the venue team.

Questions

Answers before visitors start searching.

Do organizers book through RetreatVenues?

No. They send an inquiry and continue directly with the venue, so there is no marketplace booking commission.

Can I search by group size?

Yes. Capacity is one of the core search needs for retreat planning.

Are venues worldwide?

The catalog includes retreat centers, hotels and villas across 50+ countries.

Can venues be added by owners?

Venue owners can choose a listing plan and submit their details to be published.

How far in advance should I book?

Plan at least 6 months ahead when possible, and closer to 12 months for popular venues or high season dates.

What should I ask before confirming?

Ask about deposit terms, cancellation policy, food, room setup, WiFi, AV, airport access and hidden fees.
