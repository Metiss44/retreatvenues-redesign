Before you choose

A better shortlist starts with the right questions.

Beautiful photos help, but a retreat venue also needs to work for the group, the schedule and the budget.

  • Group size, rooms, bathrooms and retreat room setup.
  • Meals, dietary needs and kitchen flexibility.
  • Airport distance, transport and arrival logistics.
  • Deposit terms, cancellation policy and hidden fees.
Questions

Before you send an inquiry.

Is RetreatVenues free for organizers?

Yes. Organizers can search venues and send inquiries without a booking fee or commission.

Do I book directly with the venue?

Yes. RetreatVenues helps you discover venues, then you continue the conversation directly with the venue.

Can I search by country or group size?

Yes. Start by destination, then narrow by venue type, capacity and key facilities.

How far in advance should I book?

Six months is a good minimum. Popular venues and high-season dates often need 12 months or more.

What should I ask before confirming?

Ask about deposit terms, cancellation policy, rooms, food, WiFi, AV, airport access and hidden fees.

Can RetreatVenues help me find options?

Yes. The team can help you build a shortlist based on your destination, group size and retreat needs.
