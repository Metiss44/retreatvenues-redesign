FAQ

Questions venue hosts usually ask.

Do you take a commission on bookings?

No. RetreatVenues charges a fixed yearly listing fee. You keep your booking revenue and manage payment directly with the organizer.

How fast will my listing be published?

Premium listings are published within 24 hours after complete venue details are received. Basic listings are published during the monthly listing schedule.

Do I need to manage a complicated account?

The page should communicate the simplified workflow clearly: you submit your information, the team prepares the listing, and changes can be requested when needed.

Who will contact my venue?

Retreat organizers and retreat leaders looking for venues by destination, group size, retreat type, amenities, and budget.
